# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, reviews
and every other project space, and to anyone who represents the project in public.

## In short

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone. We expect you to:

- be respectful and kind, and welcome people of every background and level of experience;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users;
- respect privacy. This project works with the school records of children: never publish anyone's personal data,
  and keep data from a Bakaláři account (names, marks, absences, messages) out of issues, pull requests and
  fixtures.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, and derogatory or discriminatory comments;
- personal or political attacks, trolling;
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer, Róbert Malovec, through the repository's
private reporting form ([Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/robco/bakalari/security/advisories/new))
with a title that starts with "Code of conduct". Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

The maintainer enforces this code of conduct following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact. Content that breaks GitHub's
terms can also be
[reported to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
